Product Detail View: one guide per tab

Listed in the same order as the tabs on the Product Detail page. Each guide uses a real customer example and a "if the customer asks" talking point.

  • Opportunity Tiers2 pages Tier thresholds, shortfall, Days of Purchase and ROI % for one product. Includes the metric formulas. Open guide
  • Baseline The volume used to calculate growth-based rebates, and how far the customer sits above it. Open guide
  • Product Share Contracted product volume versus competitor volume for one customer. Open guide
  • History Quarter-over-quarter discount and rebate trend, with quarterly and monthly charts. Open guide
  • Ship To NDC Drill from product and NDC down to ship-to account and invoice detail, with the rebate-eligible flag. Open guide
  • Off Contract Sales Invoice-level detail for purchases made outside the GPO contract. Open guide
  • Multi Quarter Rebate contracts that span multiple quarters, including rolling consecutive-quarter contracts. Open guide

Top Opportunities View

The portfolio-wide page for deciding where extra purchases pay off most.

  • Opportunity Highlights3 pages Ranks every product and tier a customer could still reach by ROI % and Days of Purchase. Covers Current vs. Projected and narrowing to a realistic opportunity. Open guide

Suggested flow for a customer conversation: open Opportunity Highlights in Projected view, cap Days of Purchase at the business days left in the quarter, then confirm each pick on that product's Opportunity Tiers tab.
